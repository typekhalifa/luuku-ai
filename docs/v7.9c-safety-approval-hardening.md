# V7.9-C — Safety & Approval Hardening

V7.9-C introduces a hard safety boundary between executive autonomy policy and execution eligibility.

## Safety classifications

- `SAFE_AUTONOMOUS` → `ALLOW`
- `APPROVAL_REQUIRED` → `APPROVAL`
- `FORBIDDEN` → `DENY`
- missing classification or malformed execution plan → `ESCALATE`

## Invariants

1. Safety may restrict an action but never grant execution authority.
2. Founder approval remains an explicit gate for consequential capabilities.
3. Forbidden capabilities are denied rather than converted into approval requests.
4. Unknown capabilities fail closed through escalation.
5. V6 remains the sole downstream execution authority.
